Interactive
installation

"movement"

University year 2
Using JavaScript code to track movement and generate live dynamic visuals with different sound depending on how the user moves. Collaboration between me and Ellen Karlsson.

A camera captures your body and picks up keypoints from head to toe. The keypoints leave a trace of lines when you move to create a visual representation of the body, linking your movement to a digital expression.

Tool:
Visual Studio Code, p5js Web Editor, Ml5.js

Brief

Code a visual expression on the theme "soundtrack of your life" using Javascript and js code libararies.

Process

Me and ellen drew inspiration from museums and installations where the body is central to creating something, where physical meets digital and what ever live movement is captured creates your soundtrack.

The idea that the user is the artist and producer of the result felt personal and democratic, and us being able to decide on the visual identity of the reaction appearing on a wall with code was thrilling.

Logotype and
visual identity

"beatbox"

University year 2
Logo design for Hjärtstartarregistret project, with campaign for displaying defibrillators inside a heart shaped box in Stockholm.

Tool:
Adobe Illustrator

Brief

Design a visual identity and campaign concept for Beat Box, a physical solution that improves access to defibrillators in public spaces. The identity should capture attention and resonate with adults in Stockholm.

Result

A simple and clean visual identity for Beat Box, combining the round typeface, a flexible logotype, and campaign "not just a box" to raise awareness of cardiac arrest.

UX / UI
design

"hjärtkartan"

University year 2
Research on how Sweden’s national registry of defibrillators (Hjärtstartarregistret) can spread awareness about their work.

Method/Tool:
Design Thinking, Figma

Brief

Needing help with their platform’s usability and awareness in the public about their work, we got the challenge to focus on modernising the service through user research, aiming to enhance accessibility, inclusivity, and speed of action in critical moments.

Group work, presented for Hjärtstartarregistret representative.

Process and result

One months work with the team following the design method "design thinking", consisting of five phases – empathize, define, ideate, prototype and lastly test. Working with user research and different brainstorming techniques we landed in protoyping and testing a map function, a simple and intuitive interface that can be easily navigated in moments of urgency.

Logotype and
visual identity

"trapp"

University year 1
Design for fictional architecture firm. Full visual identity including logo, colorscheme and copywrite by me.

Tool:
Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma

Brief

Logo design for a fictional architecture firm, centered on typographic elements – typeface, letterforms, and spatial arrangement paired with a complementary symbol usable alone or with the wordmark for flexible applications.

Result

The symbol, a stylized staircase in two shades of green, interacts with a clean uppercase Suisse Int’l Bold wordmark. Angled inward, the staircase conveys a welcoming, “coming home” feeling, but its orientation can be adapted in campaign material to symbolize upward progress or other narratives.
